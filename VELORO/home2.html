YOUR QUESTIONS, ANSWERED

Before the big day.

Everything you need to know before exploring our wedding and celebration formalwear atelier.

Ask A Personal Stylist
Can I enquire about wedding and reception looks?

Yes. Use our Buy Now demo order request or submit an enquiry specifying your event date, venue and preferred silhouette. Our showroom concierge will review measurements and fabric options.

Are prices and sizes live and available for instant purchase?

The storefront is currently presented in interactive demonstration mode. Prices in INR and inventory are indicative demo figures. Final purchase confirmation and payment arrangements occur directly through the showroom concierge.

Do you offer bespoke alteration and custom tailoring?

Yes, all VELORO suiting and trousers include complimentary alteration consultation at our showroom to ensure clean sleeve reveals, waist adjustment and custom trouser inseam breaks.

What should I wear as a wedding guest to a cocktail reception?

A tailored dark suit in midnight navy or charcoal, paired with a white spread-collar dress shirt and a textured grenadine tie, is universally appropriate and impeccably poised.

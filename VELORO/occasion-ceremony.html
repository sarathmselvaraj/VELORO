RULE NO. 01

Respect the Setting

For outdoor lawn or palace courtyard ceremonies, choose warm neutrals like sandstone and ecru that reflect heat and harmonise with floral decor.

RULE NO. 02

Prioritise Breathability

Long rituals require temperature regulation. A linen-silk blend offers natural ventilation while retaining clean crease lines across photographs.

RULE NO. 03

Refined Simplicity

Allow the tailoring to take center stage. Avoid overly reflective synthetics in favour of genuine mother-of-pearl buttons and matte silk neckwear.

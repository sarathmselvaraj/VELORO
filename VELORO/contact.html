GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently asked questions.

Can't find what you're looking for? Send us a note above and we'll help you find the right direction.

Can I enquire about a particular product or size?

Yes. Open a product's Quick View, choose your preferred size and select Enquire. The contact form will be prefilled with your item details. Stock and pricing need confirmation from the real store.

Can I visit the showroom without an appointment?

Showroom visit arrangements have not yet been confirmed for this demonstration. Once the store address, phone number and opening hours are supplied, this page can include the correct visit guidance.

Do you offer wedding and occasionwear?

The featured collection includes business suits, versatile blazers, elegant shirts and special-occasion styling. Explore the Wedding & Occasion category to see example options.

Are prices and sizes guaranteed?

No. The collection and its Indian prices are sample content for the website template. Please confirm availability, fit options and current selling prices with the store before purchase.

Can I place an order online?

Yes! VELORO now features an interactive Buy Now demo order request flow across the entire catalogue. Select any piece, choose your preferred size and colour, and submit an order request with your delivery address. Our showroom concierge will review your measurements and contact you to confirm details.

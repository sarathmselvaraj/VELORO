Privacy information.

This page describes the behaviour of the demonstration template and must be replaced with a business-specific policy before launch.

About this demonstration

VELORO is a front-end formalwear catalogue template. It has no live sales, back-end contact service, analytics, or newsletter mailing list. Product enquiry and newsletter forms demonstrate validation, but do not transmit or save their submitted content.

Preferences stored on this device

This template uses localStorage to remember the selected light/dark appearance and left-to-right/right-to-left reading direction. These preferences stay in the browser unless the visitor removes their site data.

Third-party services

The contact page includes a Google Maps frame configured to show a generic map of India rather than a specific business location. Map providers may collect information subject to their own terms when the map is loaded.

Before commercial launch

The store owner should supply verified business details, publish a suitable privacy policy for their jurisdiction, review map and third-party resources, and configure any future contact or marketing services with appropriate notices and consent.

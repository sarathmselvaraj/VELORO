RULE NO. 01

Embrace Midnight Navy

In low evening lighting, midnight navy appears darker and richer than true black, making it the preferred choice for red carpet ceremonies.

RULE NO. 02

Proportion of Lapels

A broad peak lapel (9.5 to 11 cm) frames your shoulders and draws the viewer's gaze upward toward your collar and smile.

RULE NO. 03

Polished Patent Footwear

Pair evening suiting with mirror-shined black Oxford shoes or velvet evening slippers. Keep leather lines pristine and understated.

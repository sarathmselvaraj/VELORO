EFFORTLESS SARTORIAL DISTINCTION

Well-dressed, never overstated.

The finest wedding guest ensemble looks completely natural. It honors the host’s milestone without competing with the groom, pairing unconstructed textured blazers with pleated wool trousers and hand-burnished leather accessories.

Ask Our Stylists for Guest Outfit Advice
RULE NO. 01

Honor the Invitation

Pay close attention to wording: "Cocktail Formal" encourages textured blazers and pocket squares; "Black Tie Optional" favours dark business suits or classic tuxedos.

RULE NO. 02

Separates with Purpose

Pairing a sandstone or camel blazer with deep navy or charcoal trousers demonstrates sartorial mastery while feeling delightfully relaxed.

RULE NO. 03

Movement & Comfort

You will be seated through banquets, standing during toasts and dancing into the night. Ensure trousers feature comfortable ease through the thigh.
